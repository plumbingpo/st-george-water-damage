How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
  • Find your main water shutoff today and make sure everyone in the house can close it.
  • Swap worn hoses feeding the washer, sinks and toilets rather than waiting for a rupture.
  • Wrap plumbing in cold, unheated parts of the house so a hard freeze cannot crack it.
  • Store valuables and important papers off the floor, especially in basements and lower levels.
  • Going away longer than a few days? Close the main water valve first.

Signs It Is Time for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A leak upstairs is sending water down the walls of the level beneath it.
  • Sewage has backed up, or the water is dark with a foul odor.
  • Pooled water is creeping over the floor into neighboring rooms or onto the staircase.
  • Trapped water overhead has made part of the ceiling bulge or sag.
  • The breaker box, a running appliance or cords along the floor sit in the path of the water.
  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.
  • An outlet, a light fixture or the ceiling above has water dripping out of it.
