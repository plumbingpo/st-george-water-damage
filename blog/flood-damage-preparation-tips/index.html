Flood Prevention for Homeowners in St. George, UT

Home / Blog / Flood Damage Preparation Tips in St. George, UT

The weather is out of your hands, but you can act early to shield a property in St. George, UT against flood damage. A prepared home takes far less of a hit when it floods, and the savings in restoration costs can reach the thousands. Below, our St. George Restoration team lays out what St. George homeowners can do to prepare most effectively.

Know Your Flood Risk

Preparation begins with knowing how likely your property is to flood. Check FEMA flood maps to determine whether your property is in a designated flood zone. Being outside a high-risk zone does not make you safe, because most flood claims are filed for properties outside the designated flood zones. Evaluate your property's topography, drainage patterns, and proximity to waterways that could overflow during heavy rain.

Physical Steps to Protect Your Home

On the physical side, keep a sump pump with a backup battery installed and maintained, store valuables and key documents higher than ground level, grade the lot to fall away from the foundation, lift electrical panels and HVAC units above where floodwater could reach, seal any foundation cracks and add a waterproof coating, and clean gutters and downspouts while running the discharge out to 6 feet or farther from the foundation.

Getting Your Insurance in Order

A typical homeowner's insurance policy excludes damage from rising floodwater. Consider purchasing flood insurance through the NFIP or a private carrier, and since there is typically a 30-day waiting period before coverage begins, don't wait for a forecast to act. For insurance purposes, take video and photos of the property and what you own. Store your policy paperwork where you can get to it, and learn your deductible and coverage limits.

Plan Your Emergency Response

Have a plan in place before flooding threatens. Keep a packed go-bag ready, and be sure of your evacuation routes. Once flooding is predicted, document how the property looks beforehand, take valuables to an upper level, and switch utilities off if you receive instructions to.
