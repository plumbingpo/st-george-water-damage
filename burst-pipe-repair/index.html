A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

When a pipe bursts in St. George or the surrounding area, St. George Restoration runs an emergency response around the clock. Our restoration technicians can help you locate and shut off your water supply, and we coordinate with licensed plumbers so the plumbing repair is done properly. Once the water source is stopped, we immediately begin extraction with truck-mounted and portable equipment, removing standing water from all affected areas as quickly as possible.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

The Benefits of Professional Burst Pipe Repair

The damage from a burst pipe tends to reach well past the wet areas in plain view. Materials get saturated in spots that can stay undiscovered for days or weeks, because the water runs along conduits, joists, floor assemblies and the insides of walls. By then, mold may already be established in hidden spaces and structural materials may be significantly weakened. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. A truck-mounted unit clears water at hundreds of times the speed of a shop vac, while a commercial dehumidifier takes 10 to 20 times more moisture out of the air than a household model. With only 24 to 48 hours before mold starts, you need equipment this fast and this strong.

Maintenance Tips for Burst Pipe Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast
  • Install water leak detection sensors near water heaters and main supply lines
  • Monitor your water pressure, because excessive pressure (above 80 PSI) strains pipes and fittings
  • Going away in cold months? Keep the thermostat running to protect the plumbing from freezing
  • Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas such as attics, garages and crawl spaces
  • Open sink cabinets and leave a slow drip running on cold nights, which helps keep pipes from freezing

Warning Signs for Burst Pipe Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Frost or ice on exposed pipes during cold weather
  • Faucets that give only a trickle, or nothing, during a freeze
  • Tap water that turns brown, yellow or cloudy
  • A spray or steady drip coming off pipework you can see
  • Damp, moldy odors around sinks, tubs or walls that hide pipes
  • Unexplained standing water along baseboards or in the middle of a room
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak
